description: Use on Mondays or when the person asks for the weekly review ("how did the week go?", "what should we change?"). Where the money works, what to stop, what to try next. Reads and suggests only.
---

1. **analyst**: the last 7 days vs the 7 before, per campaign: spend, trials, cost per trial; the top 10
   wasted searches; any disapprovals. Write `reports/week-<monday>.md`.
2. **optimizer**: up to 3 suggestions for the inbox, including a budget change only if the limits allow it.
3. Tell the person in 5 to 8 lines: what worked, what to stop, what to try, how many suggestions are waiting.
